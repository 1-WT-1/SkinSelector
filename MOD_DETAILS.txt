[center][b]Skin Selector[/b][/center]

Apply custom paint jobs to individual ships in your fleet.

[center][u]Features:[/u][/center]

 * [b]Per-Ship Skins[/b] - Assign different paint jobs to each ship, even for ships of the same model.
 * [b]Dedicated Folders[/b] - Store PNG skins in [code]user://skins/<model>/[/code].

[center][u]How to Add Skins:[/u][/center]

 1. Open the Tuning menu at Enceladus Prime.
 2. Select the [b]Paint[/b] row.
 3. Click the [b]Folder[/b] button to open the skin folder for your ship model.
 4. Copy your custom [code].png[/code] skin files into this folder.
 5. Click the [b]Refresh[/b] button in the Tuning menu to update the list.
 6. Select your skin from the dropdown list.
